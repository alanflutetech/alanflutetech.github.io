The Work

Mechanics are the floor,
not the ceiling

Most repair work stops when the pads seal and the action moves freely.

That's where I start.

I work through the mechanism. Then I play it. Listen for what it's trying to do. Bring that into alignment with how it feels under the hands. Care, not force. Presence, not urgency.

About

Over fifteen years
listening to instruments

Started in Texas. Moved to Hawaii, where the islands gave me the foundation this work is built on. Gary Harra's mentorship shaped the last four years of that chapter. Then California, Muramatsu certified under Paul Rabinov, Straubinger certified studying directly with Joel Straubinger.

Now I'm returning to Hawaii. I left searching. I'm coming back with something to give.

I come from music. Clarinet. My father was a music educator. The technical and the musical have never been separate things for me.
